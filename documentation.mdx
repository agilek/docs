# AudioGuideKit: Because Paper Maps belong in Museums

Welcome to **AudioGuideKit**. 

If you’ve ever been to a museum and handed a device that looks like a 1990s brick phone with a rubber antenna, you know why we built this. AudioGuideKit is for people who want to build beautiful, fast, and offline-capable audio tours using the technology people actually carry: their smartphones.

### The Joel Test for AudioGuideKit
1. Can you set it up in less than 2 minutes? **Yes.**
2. Does it work offline in a stone cathedral? **Yes.**
3. Can you change the theme without touching a single line of logic? **Yes.**
4. Does it support every language from English to Klingon? **Technically, yes.**

### What’s inside?
- **Vite + React**: Because we like things that are fast.
- **Styled Components**: For people who care about how things look.
- **Zero-Config Discovery**: Drop a JSON file, and the app "just knows."
